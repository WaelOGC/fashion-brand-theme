## 1. Final Brand Name

**WREN WOLD**

Contemporary European fashion brand.

| Dimension | Direction |
|-----------|-----------|
| Brand name | WREN WOLD |
| Primary market | Netherlands |
| Future markets | Europe and international |
| Positioning | Accessible premium |
| Character | Editorial, natural, human |
| Business model (initial) | Supplier-based / dropshipping |
| Long-term objective | Recognizable international fashion brand |
| Target customer | Modern women, approximately 20–40, Netherlands-first |
| Platform | WordPress + WooCommerce (custom theme) |

The brand should feel:

- Editorial in character
- Accessible premium in positioning
- Natural and human in atmosphere
- Quietly confident
- Distinctive but restrained
- Clear in commerce
- Strong in photography, typography, composition, and whitespace

---

## 2. Name Meaning

### WREN

A small bird associated with character, movement, lightness, and presence without spectacle.

### WOLD

An old English word referring to open or elevated countryside / landscape.

### Combined interpretation

**WREN WOLD** — *“A small presence in a wide world.”*

This is a **conceptual brand interpretation**, not a literal historical translation.

**Do not claim** that WREN WOLD is an ancient historical phrase, or that it has a traditional English origin as a combined expression.

### What the combination represents

- Small details within a wider world
- Individuality without loudness
- Natural character
- Openness
- Quiet confidence

---

## 3. Core Brand Idea

**QUIET CHARACTER**

The brand should feel distinctive without trying too hard.

Quiet Character means:

- Restraint over spectacle
- Presence without noise
- Confidence without luxury cliché
- Individuality without aggression

## 5. Brand Promise

### Primary Brand Promise

**“Thoughtfully made fashion for a life well lived.”**

### Supporting principle

The clothing should feel considered, versatile, relevant, and made to be worn repeatedly — not treated as disposable seasonal fashion.

---

## 6. Tone of Voice

### Tone

Calm · Clear · Intelligent · Warm · Confident · Human

### Writing principles

- Short, confident sentences
- No exaggerated luxury language
- No artificial exclusivity
- No aggressive sales language
- No empty marketing claims
- Avoid generic fashion clichés
- Communicate quality through clarity rather than hype

### Example

| Avoid | Prefer |
|-------|--------|
| “The ultimate luxury experience.” | “Made to be worn often.” |

---

## 7. Positioning

WREN WOLD sits at **accessible premium with editorial intelligence**:

- Closer to contemporary European lifestyle and accessible fashion
- Influenced by editorial thinking and premium sensibility
- Not high luxury, not avant-garde spectacle, not mass fast fashion

The digital experience must earn trust for a new brand while remaining memorable enough to grow into a recognizable international fashion identity.

## 8. Logo Direction

The approved logo direction is based on a **custom W monogram**.

### Concept

The monogram combines:

- The letter **W**
- The **WREN WOLD** initials
- A subtle abstract wing / movement gesture inspired by the wren

### Internal symbolism

The **W** also naturally represents the founder’s first initial, **W**.

This personal meaning is **internal brand symbolism** and does not need to be communicated publicly.

### What the logo must not become

- A literal bird illustration
- A complicated emblem
- A decorative luxury crest
- An overly detailed symbol

### Visual principle

**“Recognizable through restraint.”**

### Logo system (summary)

| Role | Form |
|------|------|
| Primary | W monogram + WREN WOLD wordmark |
| Secondary | W monogram alone |
| Wordmark | WREN WOLD |
| Utility | Monogram for favicon, social avatar, labels, buttons, packaging, garment labels |

Full usage rules: see `LOGO_GUIDELINES.md`.

**Important:** Concept / reference artwork is not production-ready vector. Do not treat generated concept images as final SVG assets.

## 9. Digital Experience Principles

The digital experience should express Quiet Character through:

1. Composed visual tension — art-directed, not template-aligned
2. Editorial pacing — chapters, not stacked blocks
3. Typography as recognition — rhythm before logo
4. Photography with context — clothing in life, not only on white
5. Motion with purpose — orient and delight without slowing purchase
6. Calm commerce confidence — clear, trustworthy shopping paths

Detailed visual consolidation: `VISUAL_IDENTITY.md`

## 10. Design Guardrails

### Must have

- Art-directed composition with controlled asymmetry
- Editorial pacing and section rhythm
- Distinctive typographic hierarchy (once fonts are selected)
- Photography that shows clothing in real context
- Intentional, fast motion that communicates meaning
- Clear commerce paths at all times
- Accessible, performant, responsive implementation
- European sensibility — calm, confident, contemporary
- Premium feel through restraint and precision
- Consistency across viewport sizes and input methods
- Trust signals: clear pricing, policies, and product information

### Must not become

- A generic dropshipping storefront
- A Shopify or marketplace template aesthetic
- Beige-minimal “Scandi clone” with no identity
- Black-and-white luxury cliché
- Predictable oversized-serif hero formulas
- Identical card grids with decorative excess
- Animation for decoration only
- Aggressive sales or countdown urgency
- Logo as the only brand element
- A copy of any identifiable reference brand
